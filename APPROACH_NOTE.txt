Architecture: a pretrained ResNet-18 backbone with a 256-D projection head learns design embeddings using batch-hard triplet loss. Preprocessing resizes/crops RGB images, applies geometric and color augmentations, then normalizes pixels. Color augmentation reduces palette dependence while preserving motifs. Balanced sampling supplies positives and negatives. At inference, embeddings are L2-normalized; cosine similarity ranks the gallery and a validation-selected threshold performs verification.
